## What this is

Thin orchestrators for the SynthOBS publication and verification workflow.
They import the tested engine (`src/synthobs`), do I/O, matplotlib, and
live-OBS coordination only — no engine math lives here. `__init__.py` makes
the directory importable as `scripts.` for the test suite. Responsibilities
(per-script table in `README.md`):

- `generate_figures.py` — renders the deterministic analytical figures +
  copies the promoted live captures into `output/figures/`.
- `obs_ws_probe.py` / `obs_scenario_probe.py` — live OBS gates over
  obs-websocket v5 (interactive instruments, intentionally not unit-tested
  modules).
- `promote_obs_evidence.py` — fail-closed promotion into the versioned
  manuscript evidence bundle (`../manuscript/assets/obs/`).
- `verify_provenance_strip.py` — LSB provenance-strip verification of a
  captured HUD PNG.
- `package_smoke.py` — installs a built wheel into a fresh venv and imports
  the stdlib-only engine in isolation.
- `audit_scholarship.py` — fail-closed audit of the scholarship ledger
  (`../docs/scholarship_sources.json`) against `../manuscript/references.bib`.
